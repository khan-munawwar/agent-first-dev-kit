Questions and requests sent to people outside the team, and their state.
Tasks that come out of answers go to `STATUS.md`, not here.

States: open (not sent), sent, answered, verified (we saw it work, with a
receipt). A claim from the other side is not "verified" until we saw it.

Before sending: re-check the docs and our own parameters, so we do not report
our own mistake (`docs/RESOURCES.md`, "Retracted gaps"). Batch questions into
one message where possible. The human sends; the agent drafts.
